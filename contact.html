Derek Smith

Chief Executive Officer

Derek Smith is the visionary leader behind InnovateTech, bringing over two decades of experience in innovation, strategic growth, and transformative leadership.

His journey began in Silicon Valley, where he worked with early-stage tech companies before founding InnovateTech in 2012. Known for his hands-on approach and passion for empowering teams, Derek believes that great ideas come from fostering collaboration and encouraging bold thinking.

Under his leadership, the company has grown globally while maintaining its core mission: delivering cutting-edge solutions that make a real impact.

Our Mission & Vision

Driving Innovation, Delivering Excellence

At InnovateTech, our mission is to empower businesses through cutting-edge technology solutions that simplify operations, drive growth, and enhance customer experiences.

We envision a world where innovation is accessible to all. Our goals include expanding our global reach, supporting sustainable development, and fostering digital transformation across industries.

With a strong focus on integrity, collaboration, and continuous improvement, we are committed to making a lasting impact in everything we do.
